階段 3 / 4 · Blech 長度

短導線可以「免疫」

原子被電子風吹走的同時，會在陽極端堆出應力、在陰極端拉出張應力 —— 這個應力梯度本身會推動原子往回流。線夠短時，回流剛好抵銷電子風，淨通量趨近於零。

Blech 免疫條件 j · L < (j · L)threshold　→　永生（immortal） j：電流密度。L：導線長度（更精確地說是兩個阻擋層之間的距離）。 乘積 j·L 是判準，不是 j 或 L 單獨。threshold 是材料與製程相依的常數，需要實測校準，不能憑空假設。

為什麼是「背向擴散」擋住了電子風

電子風把原子往陽極端推，陽極端原子堆積、產生壓應力；陰極端原子被掏空、產生張應力。 這個應力梯度本身會驅動原子從高應力（陽極）往低應力（陰極）擴散 —— 方向剛好跟電子風相反。 線越短，同樣的原子通量造成的應力梯度越陡（應力集中在更短的距離裡），背向擴散就越強， 直到兩者打平，淨通量歸零。

關鍵直覺

這不是「線短所以電流密度低」的錯覺 —— j 可以一樣高。 真正在保護短線的是應力梯度能不能在給定長度裡建立起足夠的背向通量，這件事只跟 j·L 有關。

互動：這條線會不會免疫

Stage 3 / 4 · Blech Length

Short wires can be "immune"

As atoms get blown away by the electron wind, they build up compressive stress at the anode end and pull tensile stress at the cathode end — and that stress gradient itself drives atoms to flow back. When the wire is short enough, the back-flow exactly cancels the electron wind, and net flux approaches zero.

The Blech immunity condition j · L < (j · L)threshold　→　immortal j: current density. L: wire length (more precisely, the distance between two blocking boundaries). The product j·L is the criterion — not j or L alone. The threshold is a material- and process-dependent constant that needs measured calibration; it can't be assumed out of thin air.

Why "back-diffusion" is what blocks the electron wind

The electron wind pushes atoms toward the anode end, where they pile up and create compressive stress; at the cathode end, atoms get hollowed out, creating tensile stress. That stress gradient itself drives atoms to diffuse from high stress (anode) toward low stress (cathode) — exactly opposite the electron wind's direction. The shorter the wire, the steeper the stress gradient produced by the same atomic flux (stress concentrates over a shorter distance), so back-diffusion gets stronger, until the two exactly balance and net flux goes to zero.

The key intuition

This isn't the illusion of "the wire is short, so current density is low" — j can stay just as high. What actually protects a short wire is whether the stress gradient can build enough back-flux within the given length, and that depends only on j·L.

誠實邊界

上面的 threshold 滑桿只是示意用的假設值，不是任何製程的實測 Blech threshold。 真實 threshold 需要在特定材料、特定阻擋層結構下實測，不同文獻報告的量級可以差好幾倍。 本頁刻意不給一個「預設 threshold」讓你套用 —— 這比 HCI／TDDB 章節的參數風險更高， 因為 Blech 免疫是一個「用了就不用再管」的假設，一旦 threshold 抓錯，等於完全誤判某段線的風險。

檢查你懂了嗎

Blech 效應在版圖上最常被用在哪裡？
大量短的 via 到下一層金屬的跳接、或是短的局部連接。這些線段如果落在 j·L 免疫區內， 理論上可以豁免傳統的 EM 電流密度上限檢查 —— 但實務上仍需搭配製程校準的 threshold， 且不同金屬層、不同阻擋層材料的 threshold 都要分開驗證，不能整片版圖套同一個數字。
如果一條線一半長度符合免疫條件、一半不符合，算不算免疫？
不算。Blech 免疫條件是針對兩個阻擋層之間連續的一段導線而言 —— 如果中間被 via 或阻擋層打斷，等效長度 L 要用打斷後的那一段，而不是整條走線的總長。 這也是為什麼版圖上刻意加短 via 分段有時能讓長線的每一小段各自落入免疫區。

Honesty note

The threshold slider above is only an illustrative assumed value — not a measured Blech threshold from any real process. A real threshold requires measurement under specific materials and specific blocking-layer structures, and the scale reported across the literature can differ by several times. This page deliberately doesn't hand you a "default threshold" to apply — this parameter carries more risk than the HCI/TDDB chapters' parameters do, because Blech immunity is a "set it and forget it" assumption: get the threshold wrong once, and you've completely misjudged that segment's risk.

Check your understanding

Where is the Blech effect most commonly exploited in layout?
Large numbers of short via hops to the next metal layer, or short local connections. If these segments fall within the j·L immunity zone, they can in principle be exempted from the conventional EM current-density ceiling check — but in practice this still needs a process-calibrated threshold, and the threshold must be verified separately for each metal layer and each blocking-layer material — you can't apply one number across the whole layout.
If half of a wire's length meets the immunity condition and half doesn't, does it count as immune?
No. The Blech immunity condition applies to one continuous stretch of wire between two blocking boundaries — if it's interrupted partway by a via or a blocking layer, the effective length L to use is the segment after the interruption, not the total length of the whole run. This is also why deliberately adding short via segmentation in a layout can sometimes let each small piece of a long line fall into the immunity zone on its own.
