環境與自我發熱Environment & self-heating

預設熱阻是校準到文獻量級的示意值，不是量測值： 14 nm bulk FinFET 實測 nominal < 3 °C、極端應力 < 18 °C；GAA 在 sub-10 nm 可達 100 K 以上。The default thermal resistance is an illustrative value calibrated to literature order-of-magnitude, not a measurement: measured 14 nm bulk FinFET is nominal < 3 °C, < 18 °C under extreme stress; GAA at sub-10 nm can reach over 100 K.

自我發熱對 HCI 的方向是反直覺的。 溫度升高 → 聲子散射變多 → 熱載子跑不遠 → 損傷率下降（這就是 HCI 的負活化能）。 所以自我發熱本身不會讓 HCI 變糟。 真正的危險在於：你在高加速條件下量到的資料被自我發熱「幫忙」壓低了， 拿去擬合出來的電壓加速因子與活化能都會偏高，外推到使用條件就偏樂觀 —— GAA 文獻的實測正是「修正自我發熱後 VAF 與 EA 都變小」。
Self-heating's effect on HCI is counter-intuitive. Higher temperature → more phonon scattering → hot carriers travel less far → the damage rate drops (this is HCI's negative activation energy). So self-heating by itself doesn't make HCI worse. The real danger is that data measured under high-acceleration conditions gets "helped" lower by self-heating, and fitting to it overstates both the voltage-acceleration factor and the activation energy, making the extrapolation to use conditions overly optimistic — GAA measurements in the literature show exactly this: "after correcting for self-heating, both VAF and EA shrink."

檢查你懂了嗎

把介電層從 SiON 換成 HfO₂、EOT 維持不變，模型裡哪些量會變、哪些不會？
不變：特徵長度 l = √(3·EOT·xj)、氧化層電容 Cox = εox/EOT、因此峰值電場 Em 與 Isub 幾乎不動 —— 這兩個量只認 EOT。
會變：物理厚度變成 EOT×(k/3.9)（漏電大幅下降），以及閘極注入位障 ΔEc 從 3.2 eV 掉到 1.4 eV，IG/ID 因此暴增。
這正是 high-k 的真面目：它解決的是穿隧漏電（看物理厚度），不是通道電場（看 EOT）。
為什麼 TiN 在下拉選單裡的真空功函數 4.33 eV 跟它在 HfO₂ 上的 4.4–4.9 eV 對不起來？
因為 4.33 eV 是「Ti 元素」的多晶真空功函數（Michaelson 1977），不是 TiN 這個化合物的，更不是 TiN 在某個疊構上的有效功函數。
真正的原因是費米能階釘扎：金屬與高k 界面的偶極與缺陷會把有效功函數往中能隙拉。同一個 ALD Pt 在 SiO₂ 上量到 5.52 eV、在 ZrO₂ 上只有 4.76 eV —— 差 0.76 eV。真空功函數只能當排序參考；設計必須用該疊構的實測 EWF。
打開自我發熱之後，界面態生成率下降了。這是好消息嗎？
對元件本身是，對你的外推不是。HCI 的活化能是負的（溫度升高 → 聲子散射變多 → 熱載子跑不遠），所以自我發熱確實會壓低瞬時損傷率。
危險在於：你在高加速條件量到的資料被自我發熱「幫忙」壓低了，拿去擬合出來的電壓加速因子與活化能都會偏高，外推到使用條件（自我發熱小得多）就會偏樂觀。GAA 文獻的實測正是「做完自我發熱修正後，VAF 與 EA 都變小」，EOL ΔIdsat% 的修正量達 10%～35%。
把 Nhalo 從 2×10¹⁷ 調到 1×10¹⁸，Em 與壽命各變多少？為什麼還要做 halo？
自己拉一次看。halo 讓空乏區縮短 → 有效特徵長度 l 變小 → 峰值電場升高 → 壽命明顯變差。
做 halo 不是為了 HCI，是為了抑制短通道效應（roll-off、DIBL、punch-through）。它是效能／短通道控制與 HCI 之間的直接取捨 —— 跟氮化在 TDDB 與 NBTI 之間的取捨是同一類問題。可靠度參數彼此耦合，單獨最佳化任何一項都會把別的弄壞。
判準表裡 Idsat 10% 的壽命遠長於 Idlin 10%。這代表 Idlin 判準比較嚴格 —— 那該用哪一個？
兩者都不是「比較對」，差別在元件在電路裡實際工作在哪一區。
損傷集中在汲極端：線性區時那一段直接落在導通路徑上（等效串聯電阻），退化被完整看見；飽和區時那一段被夾止區遮住，退化被稀釋 —— 所以 ΔIdlin 天生就是 ΔIdsat 的幾倍。
判準應該跟著應用走：差動對、電流鏡、cascode、共源放大、主動負載這類飽和區元件，功能參數是 Idsat、gm 與匹配漂移，卡 Idlin 沒有對應的電路後果；傳輸閘、開關、導通電阻型應用才該卡 Idlin。這張表可以直接拿去跟 RD／business 說明「同一份資料，換判準差幾倍」。
這一頁選了 FinFET 或 GAA，算出來的數字可以拿去用嗎？
不可以。電靜學核心仍然是平面 pseudo-2D 模型 —— 選結構改變的是預設參數、加速指數、自我發熱熱阻與說明文字，不是換成該架構的三維解。
它能給你的是方向與量級：哪一個旋鈕最有力、m 從 2.9 掉到 1.1 會讓外推差多少、自我發熱怎麼污染擬合。真正的數字必須來自你自己的量測與 TCAD。

Check your understanding

Switch the dielectric from SiON to HfO₂ while keeping EOT fixed — which quantities in the model change, and which don't?
Unchanged: the characteristic length l = √(3·EOT·xj), the oxide capacitance Cox = εox/EOT, and so the peak field Em and Isub barely move — both of these quantities only care about EOT.
Changed: the physical thickness becomes EOT×(k/3.9) (leakage drops sharply), and the gate-injection barrier ΔEc falls from 3.2 eV to 1.4 eV, so IG/ID shoots up.
This is high-k's real story: it solves tunneling leakage (which cares about physical thickness), not the channel field (which cares about EOT).
Why doesn't TiN's vacuum work function of 4.33 eV in the dropdown match its 4.4–4.9 eV on HfO₂?
Because 4.33 eV is the polycrystalline vacuum work function of "elemental Ti" (Michaelson 1977) — not of the compound TiN, and certainly not TiN's effective work function on some particular stack.
The real cause is Fermi-level pinning: dipoles and defects at the metal/high-k interface pull the effective work function toward midgap. The very same ALD Pt measures 5.52 eV on SiO₂ but only 4.76 eV on ZrO₂ — a 0.76 eV difference. The vacuum work function is only a rough ranking reference; a design must use the measured EWF for its actual stack.
After turning on self-heating, the interface-trap generation rate drops. Is that good news?
For the device itself, yes; for your extrapolation, no. HCI's activation energy is negative (higher temperature → more phonon scattering → hot carriers travel less far), so self-heating genuinely does suppress the instantaneous damage rate.
The danger: data measured under high-acceleration conditions gets "helped" lower by self-heating, so fitting to it overstates both the voltage-acceleration factor and the activation energy, making the extrapolation to use conditions (where self-heating is much smaller) overly optimistic. GAA measurements in the literature show exactly this — "after correcting for self-heating, both VAF and EA shrink" — with the correction to EOL ΔIdsat% reaching 10%–35%.
Turn Nhalo from 2×10¹⁷ up to 1×10¹⁸ — how much do Em and lifetime change? So why bother with halo at all?
Try it yourself. Halo shortens the depletion region → the effective characteristic length l shrinks → the peak field rises → lifetime clearly worsens.
Halo isn't there for HCI — it's there to suppress short-channel effects (roll-off, DIBL, punch-through). It's a direct trade-off between performance/short-channel control and HCI — the same category of problem as nitridation's trade-off between TDDB and NBTI. Reliability parameters are coupled to each other; optimizing any one in isolation breaks another.
In the criteria table, the lifetime at Idsat 10% is far longer than at Idlin 10%. Does that mean the Idlin criterion is stricter — so which one should be used?
Neither one is "more correct" — the difference comes down to which region the device actually operates in within the circuit.
The damage is concentrated at the drain: in the linear region, that spot sits directly in the conduction path (as equivalent series resistance), so the degradation is fully visible; in saturation, that spot is shielded by the pinch-off region, so the degradation is diluted — which is why ΔIdlin is inherently several times ΔIdsat.
The criterion should follow the application: for saturation-region devices like differential pairs, current mirrors, cascodes, common-source amplifiers, and active loads, the functional parameters are Idsat, gm, and matching drift — gating on Idlin has no corresponding circuit consequence; transmission gates, switches, and Ron-type applications are the ones that should gate on Idlin. This table is something you can take straight to R&D or business stakeholders to show "the same data, off by however many times depending on which criterion you pick."
This page has FinFET or GAA selected — can the resulting numbers actually be used?
No. The electrostatic core is still the planar pseudo-2D model — selecting a structure changes the default parameters, the acceleration exponents, the self-heating thermal resistance, and the explanatory text, not the underlying calculation into that architecture's real 3D solution.
What it can give you is direction and order of magnitude: which knob has the most leverage, how much the extrapolation shifts when m drops from 2.9 to 1.1, how self-heating contaminates a fit. The real numbers have to come from your own measurements and TCAD.
