偏壓溫度不穩定性（NBTI/PBTI）
可靠度物理，從零開始

NBTI 發生在 pMOS 通道反轉、閘極負偏壓、沒有汲極電流的靜態應力下 —— 應力參數跟 HCI 完全不同：不用開關、不用電流，一顆待機中的電晶體也在慢慢老化。 更麻煩的是移除應力後訊號會自發部分回復，這件事直接影響你怎麼量測它。 這份教材分五個階段：直覺、Si-H 斷裂與 R-D 模型、恢復動力學與量測陷阱、時間指數 n 之爭、以及判準與防治。 每一頁都短，一次只講一件事，卡住了隨時可以往回翻。

一句話版本

負偏壓下電洞在 Si/介電層界面累積，界面處鈍化用的 Si–H 鍵被電場與電洞共同打斷， 留下的懸鍵變成界面態，直接推動 |Vt| 上升。停止應力後，一部分界面態會自發鈍化回復 —— 量測本身的延遲就足以低估真實劣化量。

Negative-Bias Temperature Instability (NBTI/PBTI)
reliability physics, from zero

NBTI happens under static stress: a pMOS channel inverted, a negative gate bias, and zero drain current — its stress parameters are completely different from HCI's: no switching, no current, and a transistor sitting idle is still slowly aging. Making things trickier, the signal spontaneously partially recovers once stress is removed, which directly shapes how you have to measure it. This course breaks it into five stages: intuition, Si-H bond breaking and the R-D model, recovery dynamics and measurement traps, the battle over the time exponent n, and finally criteria with mitigation. Every page is short and covers exactly one idea — you can always flip back.

The 30-second version

Under negative bias, holes accumulate at the Si/dielectric interface, and the Si–H bonds used to passivate that interface get broken by the combined action of the field and the holes. The dangling bonds left behind become interface traps, directly driving |Vt| up. Once stress stops, a fraction of those interface traps spontaneously re-passivate and recover — the delay in measurement itself is enough to underestimate the true degradation.
