電子風把原子吹走

EM 不是熱效應。是導電電子跟金屬晶格原子不斷碰撞，把動量交給原子， 在夠高的電流密度下，這股「風」足以讓原子沿電流方向淨移動。

一分鐘版

原子從陰極端（電子流入端）被推向陽極端（電子流出端）。陰極端因此越來越空洞， 最後斷路；陽極端則可能因原子堆積擠出突起，碰到旁邊的線就短路。失效位置幾乎都在陰極端。

「風」是怎麼吹的

金屬導線裡的原子被離子核的靜電力（direct force）往電子流的反方向拉， 同時被大量高速電子的動量碰撞（electron wind force）往電子流方向推。 在正常金屬（Al、Cu）裡，electron wind force 遠大於 direct force， 淨效果是原子被推著跟電子跑同一個方向 —— 也就是跟傳統電流方向相反。

關鍵直覺

原子搬家的方向 = 電子流動的方向（不是傳統電流方向）。 陰極（電子流出金屬進入下一段的那一端）被掏空，陽極端被原子堆積。

Stage 1 / 4 · Intuition

The electron wind blows atoms away

EM isn't a thermal effect. Conducting electrons keep colliding with the atoms of the metal lattice, handing over momentum, and at a high enough current density, that "wind" is strong enough to produce a net drift of atoms along the current direction.

The 1-minute version

Atoms get pushed from the cathode end (where electrons flow in) toward the anode end (where electrons flow out). The cathode end grows emptier and emptier, eventually opening the circuit; the anode end may pile atoms up into a hillock that shorts to a neighboring line. Failure almost always happens at the cathode end.

How the "wind" actually blows

Atoms in a metal wire are pulled against the electron-flow direction by the electrostatic pull of the ion cores (the direct force), while simultaneously being pushed along the electron-flow direction by momentum transfer from the huge number of fast-moving electrons (the electron wind force). In ordinary metals (Al, Cu), the electron wind force vastly outweighs the direct force, so the net effect is that atoms get pushed along with the electrons — i.e., opposite to the direction of conventional current.

The key intuition

The direction atoms move = the direction electrons flow (not the direction of conventional current). The cathode (the end where electrons leave this segment of metal for the next) gets hollowed out; the anode end accumulates atoms.

檢查你懂了嗎

為什麼失效幾乎都發生在陰極端，不是陽極端？
陰極端是原子被「搬走」的那一端 —— 原子濃度持續下降直到形成空洞、電流路徑被截斷， 這是一個會自我加劇的過程（空洞讓局部電流密度更集中，加速空洞成長）。 陽極端雖然原子堆積、可能形成突起造成短路，但這通常需要更長時間，也不是每次都會碰到鄰線。
為什麼說 EM「不是熱效應」？
溫度在 EM 裡的角色是加速擴散（透過 Arrhenius 的 exp(−Ea/kT) 項）， 但驅動原子淨位移方向的是電流帶來的動量傳遞，不是溫度梯度本身。這跟自我發熱在 HCI 裡的角色不一樣 —— 自我發熱對 EM 是單純加速，沒有 HCI 那種「方向相反」的複雜性。
Al 跟 Cu 的失效模式為什麼會不一樣？
兩者主要擴散路徑不同：Al 幾乎完全靠晶界擴散（因為 Al 表面會生成緻密氧化層， 封住表面擴散路徑）；Cu 的表面／介面不容易形成保護性氧化層， 所以 Cu/覆蓋層介面反而常常是比晶界更弱的路徑 —— 這件事直接決定了下一階段材料活化能表的結構。

Check your understanding

Why does failure almost always happen at the cathode end, not the anode?
The cathode end is where atoms get "carried away" — the atom concentration keeps dropping until a void forms and the current path is cut, and this is a self-accelerating process (the void concentrates local current density further, speeding up its own growth). The anode end does accumulate atoms and may form a hillock that shorts, but that usually takes longer and doesn't always happen to reach a neighboring line.
Why is EM said to be "not a thermal effect"?
Temperature's role in EM is to accelerate diffusion (through the Arrhenius term exp(−Ea/kT)), but what drives the direction of net atomic displacement is momentum transfer from the current, not a temperature gradient itself. That's different from self-heating's role in HCI — for EM, self-heating is a pure accelerant, without the "opposite direction" complexity HCI has.
Why do Al and Cu fail differently?
Their dominant diffusion paths differ: Al relies almost entirely on grain-boundary diffusion (because Al forms a dense native oxide on its surface, sealing off the surface diffusion path); Cu's surface/interface doesn't easily form a protective oxide, so the Cu/capping-layer interface is often actually a weaker path than the grain boundary — this single fact directly shapes the structure of the material activation-energy table in the next stage.
