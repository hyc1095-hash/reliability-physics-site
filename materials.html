可調模型裡的每一個下拉選項背後都是這一頁的數字。 規則只有一條：照抄來源，不平均、不調和。來源之間有分歧就兩個都列，查不到就寫「未查證」。

先講三件容易踩的事
  • 「Ti 的功函數」不是「TiN 的功函數」，更不是「TiN 在 HfO₂ 上的有效功函數」。 這三個數字差很多，而且只有第三個能拿來設計。
  • 同一個金屬放在不同介電層上，有效功函數可以差 0.76 eV。 ALD Pt 在 SiO₂ 上 5.52 eV、在 ZrO₂ 上 4.76 eV —— 這就是費米能階釘扎。
  • 能帶偏移的文獻分歧比你想像的大。SiO₂ 的 ΔEc 有 3.2 / 3.3 / 3.5 三種說法， HfO₂ 有 1.4 / 1.48 / 1.5 / 1.9。用在穿隧位障計算時，這 0.5 eV 會放大成數量級。

Material & Structure Reference Table

Every dropdown option in the tunable models on this site traces back to a number on this page. There's only one rule: copy the source exactly — no averaging, no reconciling. Where sources disagree, list both; where nothing could be found, mark it "unverified."

Three easy traps, up front
  • "Ti's work function" is not "TiN's work function," and it's definitely not "TiN's effective work function on HfO₂." These three numbers differ substantially, and only the third one is usable for actual design.
  • The same metal on different dielectrics can have an effective work function that differs by 0.76 eV. ALD Pt measures 5.52 eV on SiO₂ and 4.76 eV on ZrO₂ — this is Fermi-level pinning.
  • Literature disagreement on band offsets is bigger than you'd expect. SiO₂'s ΔEc has three reported values — 3.2 / 3.3 / 3.5 — and HfO₂ has four: 1.4 / 1.48 / 1.5 / 1.9. When used in a tunneling-barrier calculation, that 0.5 eV spread gets amplified by orders of magnitude.

3　元件結構的 HCI 差異

Why can't the vacuum work function be used for design? A dipole forms at the metal/high-k interface, and defects in the dielectric (oxygen vacancies in HfO₂) pin the Fermi level, pulling the effective work function toward midgap. The Pt row already demonstrates this: the same metal differs by 0.76 eV depending on the dielectric. The vacuum work function is only useful as a rough ranking reference; design must use the measured EWF for that specific stack, thickness, and anneal condition.

4　HCI 的統計與外推慣例

The literature range for the time exponent n is wide: 0.1 (pure charge trapping at existing defects) to 0.6 (pure interface-trap generation). Room-temperature planar devices are commonly measured at 0.55–0.78; about 0.32–0.49 at 77 K; about 0.30 at 4.2 K. A small n means the dominant mechanism is charge trapping at pre-existing dielectric defects, not new interface-trap generation.

Self-heating's direction needs to be stated carefully. The direct effect of a higher temperature is more scattering of hot carriers, so they travel less far — the damage rate drops (this is HCI's negative activation energy; both SEMATECH and NASA/JPL give Ea ≈ −0.1 to −0.2 eV). When the literature says self-heating "makes HCD worse," what it's actually describing is an artifact of measurement and extrapolation: data suppressed by self-heating during stress produces a fitted voltage-acceleration factor and activation energy that both run high, making the extrapolation to use conditions look overly optimistic. The GAA paper's measured conclusion is exactly that "after the self-heating correction, both VAF and EA decrease."
