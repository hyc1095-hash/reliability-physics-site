階段 2 / 4 · Black 方程式與材料活化能

業界標準壽命模型，以及「材料到底怎麼選」

Black 方程式把電流密度與溫度兩個應力參數整理成一個壽命式。 但式子裡的 Ea 高度材料與製程相依 —— 這一頁把常見金屬系統的晶界擴散與介面擴散活化能整理成表， 這也是你在製程選擇（Al vs Cu、介電質 cap vs 金屬 cap）時真正在動的那個參數。

Black 方程式 MTF = A · J−n · exp( Ea / kT ) MTF：中位數失效時間。A：製程相關前置係數（未校準，見下方誠實邊界）。 n：電流密度指數，典型 1（純空洞成核主導）～2（經典空洞成長主導，設計上常保守取 2）。 Ea：主導擴散路徑的活化能 —— 這是材料選擇真正影響的參數。

Stage 2 / 4 · The Black Equation & Material Activation Energy

The industry-standard lifetime model, and "how to actually pick a material"

The Black equation folds two stress parameters — current density and temperature — into a single lifetime formula. But the Ea inside it is highly material- and process-dependent — this page tables the grain-boundary and interface diffusion activation energies for common metal systems, which is also the exact parameter you're actually moving when you choose a process (Al vs. Cu, dielectric cap vs. metal cap).

The Black equation MTF = A · J−n · exp( Ea / kT ) MTF: median time to failure. A: a process-dependent prefactor (uncalibrated — see the honesty note below). n: the current-density exponent, typically 1 (pure void-nucleation-dominated) to 2 (classic void-growth-dominated; design work often conservatively uses 2). Ea: the activation energy of the dominant diffusion path — this is the parameter material choice actually affects.

What this table is really saying

Al→Al-Cu is "plug the grain boundary"; Cu's dielectric cap→metal cap is "plug the interface" — both process advances follow the exact same logic: upgrade whichever diffusion path currently has the lowest Ea to the next-lowest one. For a sense of scale: in the Black equation, each roughly 0.1 eV increase in Ea raises MTF at 105°C by about 30–50% (the exponential term's order of magnitude).

檢查你懂了嗎

為什麼設計上常保守取 n = 2，即使有些文獻報告 n ≈ 1？
n = 1 通常對應「空洞已經存在、只是在成核」的階段，n = 2 對應經典的空洞穩態成長模型。 保守取 n = 2 代表假設對電流密度更敏感，在同樣的良率目標下算出來的允許電流密度上限會更嚴（更保守）。 用較小的 n 去外推等於假設系統對電流密度不那麼敏感，容易高估允許的電流密度。
Cu 明明活化能比 Al 高，為什麼還要加金屬 cap？不是已經比 Al 好了嗎？
「比 Al 好」是相對於 Al 的晶界擴散（0.4–0.5 eV）而言。但 Cu 用介電質 cap 時的介面擴散 （0.8–1.06 eV）其實沒有比 Al-Cu 合金的晶界擴散（0.6–0.95 eV）好太多 —— Cu 真正的優勢要靠金屬 cap 把介面路徑堵住之後才充分發揮出來（可以到 1.5–2.2 eV 量級）。這也是為什麼業界會為了 EM 額外多做一道 CoWP／CoSnP 電鍍製程，即使它會犧牲一些 RC。
同樣是「金屬 cap」，為什麼活化能可以從 0.72 到 2.2 eV 差這麼多？
因為表格裡那組數據刻意控制了晶粒結構這個變因：同一種 cap 材料，晶粒結構從近竹狀（每個晶粒橫跨整個線寬， 幾乎沒有晶界可以當擴散路徑）到純多晶（晶界密集）之間，量到的活化能差了 3 倍。 金屬 cap 只解決介面路徑；晶界路徑的強弱仍然由晶粒結構決定 —— 兩者是獨立的變因，要一起控制才有效。

Check your understanding

Why does design work often conservatively use n = 2, even though some literature reports n ≈ 1?
n = 1 usually corresponds to a stage where "voids already exist and are merely nucleating"; n = 2 corresponds to the classic steady-state void-growth model. Conservatively choosing n = 2 means assuming greater sensitivity to current density, which produces a stricter (more conservative) upper bound on allowed current density for the same yield target. Extrapolating with a smaller n implicitly assumes the system is less sensitive to current density, which tends to overestimate the allowed current density.
Cu already has a higher activation energy than Al — why bother adding a metal cap? Isn't it already better than Al?
"Better than Al" is relative to Al's grain-boundary diffusion (0.4–0.5 eV). But Cu's interface diffusion under a dielectric cap (0.8–1.06 eV) isn't actually much better than an Al-Cu alloy's grain-boundary diffusion (0.6–0.95 eV) — Cu's real advantage only comes out fully once a metal cap plugs the interface path (reaching the 1.5–2.2 eV range). That's exactly why industry adds an extra CoWP/CoSnP plating step purely for EM, even though it costs some RC.
Both are called "metal cap" — why does the activation energy swing all the way from 0.72 to 2.2 eV?
Because that dataset deliberately controls for grain structure as a variable: with the same cap material, as grain structure moves from near-bamboo (each grain spans the full line width, leaving almost no grain boundary to serve as a diffusion path) to pure polycrystalline (dense grain boundaries), the measured activation energy differs by 3×. A metal cap only solves the interface path; the strength of the grain-boundary path is still set by grain structure — the two are independent variables, and both must be controlled together to be effective.
