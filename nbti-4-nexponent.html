Stage 4 / 5

The battle over the time exponent n:
the longest-running argument in reliability-physics literature

Stage 2 told you the R-D model predicts n = 1/6 (H₂ diffusion) or 1/4 (atomic H diffusion), and measurements land around 0.15–0.25. This page tells you something messier: that "gap" isn't measurement error — it's an academic dispute running from before 2003 to today, between two heavyweight camps that still haven't fully reconciled. The argument isn't about who's better at running experiments. It's that the same set of numbers can be read as both "the R-D model is right" and "the R-D model fundamentally can't explain this". By the end of this page you'll see why Stage 3's measurement trap isn't just "measurement is a bit annoying" — it once sent an entire field down the wrong diffusing-species conclusion for the better part of a decade.

① n 不是一個數，是一場公案

R-D 模型的核心邏輯很簡單：介面 Si-H 鍵斷裂（反應）放出氫物種，氫物種往閘極介電層內部擴散。 n 的理論值完全由「哪一種氫物種在擴散」決定，不同物種給出完全不同的指數：

H⁺（質子）n = 1/2漂移而非擴散主導；這個指數從未在 NBTI 實測中出現過
H（原子）n = 1/4pre-2003 文獻的「經典」指數，大量實測與此量級相符
H₂（分子）n = 1/6 ≈ 0.167擴散較慢，指數較小

問題出在：n 的理論值指向一種物種，但另一個獨立量到的物理量——活化能——指向另一種物種。 Alam 等人在他們的回顧論文裡把這個矛盾講得非常白：

pre-2003 NBTI 文獻的核心難題

“This is the core dilemma of pre-2003 NBTI literature: the time-exponent in Eq. (3) suggests atomic H diffusion, while temperature activation suggests H₂ diffusion.”

翻成白話：量到的時間指數 n≈0.25 說是氫原子在擴散；量到的活化能 EA≈0.5 eV 卻說是氫分子在擴散。 兩個獨立量到的數字，指向兩個互斥的機制——這不是哪個量錯了，是整套詮釋架構本身就有裂縫。

而 n = 1/2（質子漂移）的下場更乾脆——理論predict 得出來，但從來沒人量到過：

一個乾淨的反例
“The n = 1/2 regime has also never been seen in NBTI measurements, leading one to conclude that NBTI degradation through proton transport is unlikely.” —— 三個候選物種，先刪掉一個。剩下 H 跟 H₂ 兩個候選， 而這兩個候選又被 n 與 EA 兩個獨立量測互相矛盾地指向。這就是階段 2 那個「理論 vs 實測落差」真正的來歷： 不是測不準，是 2003 年之前整個領域對「哪個物種在擴散」這件事本身就沒有一致答案。

① n isn't one number, it's an unfinished dispute

The R-D model's core logic is simple: Si-H bonds break at the interface (reaction), releasing hydrogen species that diffuse into the gate dielectric. The theoretical value of n is entirely determined by which hydrogen species is doing the diffusing, and different species give completely different exponents:

H⁺ (proton)n = 1/2Drift-dominated rather than diffusion; this exponent has never shown up in NBTI measurements
H (atomic)n = 1/4The "classic" pre-2003 exponent, matched by a large body of measurements at that order of magnitude
H₂ (molecular)n = 1/6 ≈ 0.167Slower diffusion, smaller exponent

The problem: the theoretical value of n points to one species, but another independently measured quantity — the activation energy — points to a different one. Alam et al. put this contradiction bluntly in their review paper:

The core dilemma of pre-2003 NBTI literature

“This is the core dilemma of pre-2003 NBTI literature: the time-exponent in Eq. (3) suggests atomic H diffusion, while temperature activation suggests H₂ diffusion.”

In plain terms: the measured time exponent n≈0.25 says atomic hydrogen is diffusing; the measured activation energy EA≈0.5 eV says it's molecular hydrogen. Two independently measured numbers point to two mutually exclusive mechanisms — neither measurement is wrong; the whole interpretive framework had a crack in it.

And n = 1/2 (proton drift) met an even cleaner fate — predicted by theory, but never once measured:

One clean counter-example
“The n = 1/2 regime has also never been seen in NBTI measurements, leading one to conclude that NBTI degradation through proton transport is unlikely.” — one of three candidate species eliminated outright. That leaves H and H₂, and those two are the ones pointed at contradictorily by two independent measurements, n and EA. This is where Stage 2's "theory-vs-measurement gap" actually comes from: it isn't measurement noise — before 2003, the field itself had no consistent answer to "which species is diffusing" in the first place.

② 量測延遲怎麼把 n「做」出來

階段 3 講過：移除應力到開始量測之間的延遲，會讓訊號系統性地回復掉一部分。 這一頁要把這件事跟 n 接起來——量測延遲不只是讓數字偏小，還會系統性地改變你擬合出來的 n 值本身。 Grasser 等人整理了文獻裡的實測 n 值，分成兩群：

同一個物理量，兩種量測方式，兩個 n

“Experimental results obtained with delayed measurements show a linear increase of the exponent with temperature […] with values around 0.2 – 0.3. In contrast, temperature-independent exponents in the range 0.07 – 0.2 have been extracted from recent delay-free measurements.”

有延遲的傳統量測：n 隨溫度線性上升，落在 0.2–0.3，而且「隨溫度變化」這件事本身就很可疑—— 一個真正由擴散動力學決定的指數，不應該對量測方法的延遲時間這麼敏感。 無延遲（on-the-fly）量測：n 落在 0.07–0.2，且不隨溫度變化——更像是在量一個真正的物理常數。

Alam 等人的立場更進一步：他們認為 n≈0.25 這個「經典」指數本身就是量測延遲造出來的假象：

「經典值」被改判為測量假象
“In fact, n ≈ 0.25 is not robust exponent, as indicated by the saturation of NBTI degradation observed by experiments […] identification of NBTI with H-diffusion is actually accidental and reflects measurement-delay induced relaxation of underlying degradation generated by H₂ diffusion. The diffusing specie is H₂ with consistent values of n ≈ 0.16.” —— 換句話說：n≈0.25 不是因為氫原子真的在擴散，是因為傳統量測延遲把 H₂ 擴散的真實曲線「切」出一個看起來像 n=1/4 的形狀。 把延遲效應扣掉，氫分子擴散（n≈0.16）才是正確答案——這是 Alam/Mahapatra 陣營的結論。

② How measurement delay "manufactures" n

Stage 3 explained that the delay between removing stress and starting measurement lets the signal systematically recover away part of itself. This page connects that to n directly — measurement delay doesn't just shrink the number, it systematically changes the fitted n value itself. Grasser et al. summarized the measured n values in the literature into two groups:

Same physical quantity, two measurement methods, two n's

“Experimental results obtained with delayed measurements show a linear increase of the exponent with temperature […] with values around 0.2 – 0.3. In contrast, temperature-independent exponents in the range 0.07 – 0.2 have been extracted from recent delay-free measurements.”

Conventional delayed measurement: n rises linearly with temperature, landing at 0.2–0.3 — and that temperature dependence is itself suspicious, since an exponent truly set by diffusion kinetics shouldn't be this sensitive to the measurement method's delay time. Delay-free (on-the-fly) measurement: n lands at 0.07–0.2 and doesn't vary with temperature — far more consistent with measuring a genuine physical constant.

Alam et al. go a step further: they argue the "classic" n≈0.25 exponent is itself an artifact manufactured by measurement delay:

The "classic value" downgraded to a measurement artifact
“In fact, n ≈ 0.25 is not robust exponent, as indicated by the saturation of NBTI degradation observed by experiments […] identification of NBTI with H-diffusion is actually accidental and reflects measurement-delay induced relaxation of underlying degradation generated by H₂ diffusion. The diffusing specie is H₂ with consistent values of n ≈ 0.16.” — in other words: n≈0.25 isn't real evidence that atomic hydrogen is diffusing; conventional measurement delay "cuts" the true H₂-diffusion curve into a shape that merely looks like n=1/4. Strip out the delay artifact and molecular H₂ diffusion (n≈0.16) is the right answer — that's the Alam/Mahapatra camp's conclusion.

③ 兩個陣營，同一個數字，兩種解讀

到這裡故事看起來快要收尾了：延遲量測的 n≈0.25 是假象，無延遲的 n≈0.15–0.16 才是真相，剛好吻合 H₂-RD 模型的理論預測 1/6—— 皆大歡喜，R-D 模型獲勝。但 Grasser、Kaczer 這邊（TU Wien / imec）不同意這個收尾方式。 他們也分析了同一批被認為「無延遲」的超快量測數據，發現就算把延遲效應修正到趨近於零，擬合出來的指數依然落在 n≈0.15：

數字對了，不代表模型對了

“The fact that some OTF measurements and the corrected MSM measurements give exponents of around n = 0.15, which is close to the value predicted by the H₂ based RD model (n = 1/6), should not let one arrive at the conclusion that the RD model is consequently reasonable.”

Grasser 的理由不是「這個數字錯了」，而是「這個數字對，不代表背後的模型對」： R-D 模型在應力階段推導出 n=1/6，靠的是正反應（Si-H 斷裂）與逆反應（氫物種回填鈍化）之間一個很精細的平衡—— 拿掉逆反應，單純正反應會直接給出 n=1。但換到恢復階段（移除應力後），同一套逆反應機制卻完全對不上實測：

“It has been clearly shown […] that the RD model predicts 80% of the relaxation to occur within 3 decades, while in reality relaxation is observed to span more than 10 decades.” —— 模型說恢復應該在 3 個十進位的時間內完成八成，實測卻要跨 10 個十進位以上才恢復完。 一個在應力階段「湊巧」給出正確指數、卻在恢復階段差了 7 個數量級的模型，數字對不對已經不是重點了。

陣營核心主張關鍵證據
Alam／MahapatraPurdue、IIT Bombay 公案已解：pre-2003 的矛盾來自量測延遲偽造出 n≈0.25；扣掉延遲，H₂ 擴散（n≈0.16）與活化能（≈0.5–0.6 eV）一致吻合 OTF 量測在多種應力條件、溫度、介電層厚度下，一致收斂到 n≈0.16（圖 7 標註「universal power-law slope」）
Grasser／KaczerTU Wien、imec 數字收斂不等於模型正確：R-D 模型的恢復行為，不管怎麼延伸都無法描述實測跨 10 decades 的恢復尾巴 以「通用恢復函數」重新分析 Reisinger 等人的超快量測，證實即使修正到零延遲，n 仍落在 0.15，但恢復行為本身與 R-D 模型系統性不符

這場公案目前沒有單一「正確答案」可以寫進教科書——這正是它值得放進教材的原因： 它示範了「兩個獨立陣營從同一批數據得出不同結論」在真實文獻裡長什麼樣子，而不是一個已經塵埃落定、可以簡化成一行公式的故事。

③ Two camps, one number, two readings

At this point the story looks almost wrapped up: the delayed-measurement n≈0.25 is an artifact, the delay-free n≈0.15–0.16 is the truth, and it happens to match the H₂-RD model's theoretical prediction of 1/6 — everyone's happy, the R-D model wins. But Grasser and Kaczer's group (TU Wien / imec) doesn't accept that ending. They analyzed the same "delay-free" ultra-fast data and found that even after correcting the delay effect down toward zero, the fitted exponent still lands at n≈0.15:

Getting the number right isn't the same as getting the model right

“The fact that some OTF measurements and the corrected MSM measurements give exponents of around n = 0.15, which is close to the value predicted by the H₂ based RD model (n = 1/6), should not let one arrive at the conclusion that the RD model is consequently reasonable.”

Grasser's reasoning isn't "that number is wrong" — it's "that number being right doesn't mean the model behind it is right": the R-D model derives n=1/6 during the stress phase through a delicate balance between the forward reaction (Si-H breaking) and the reverse reaction (hydrogen species diffusing back and re-passivating) — strip out the reverse reaction and the forward reaction alone gives n=1 outright. But switch to the relaxation phase (after stress is removed), and that very same reverse-reaction mechanism fails to match measurement at all:

“It has been clearly shown […] that the RD model predicts 80% of the relaxation to occur within 3 decades, while in reality relaxation is observed to span more than 10 decades.” — the model says 80% of recovery should finish within 3 decades of time; measurement shows it takes more than 10 decades to finish recovering. Once a model "happens" to get the right exponent during stress but misses by 7 orders of magnitude during relaxation, whether the number is right stops being the point.

This dispute currently has no single "correct answer" you could write into a textbook — and that's exactly why it belongs in this coursework: it shows what "two independent camps reaching different conclusions from the same data" actually looks like in real literature, rather than a settled story that can be reduced to one tidy formula.

⑤ 這對你的意義：為什麼外推只能用應力期間的 n

這把階段 2、3、4 串成一條線：階段 2 給你 R-D 理論值；階段 3 告訴你量測延遲會系統性低估訊號； 這一頁告訴你延遲不只是讓數字變小，還會系統性地把 n 往上推（因為早期數據點被恢復吃掉的比例更高，擬合出的斜率會變陡）。 三件事合起來，變成一條清楚的工程規則：

外推用的 n，只能來自應力期間擬合，不能來自移除應力後的殘餘訊號反推
不管你相信 Alam／Mahapatra 的「H₂ 擴散、公案已解」，還是 Grasser／Kaczer 的「數字對、模型未必對」， 兩個陣營在這一條工程規則上沒有分歧：只要量測方法本身帶有延遲，n 就會被系統性地污染往高估的方向， 外推到 10 年的 ΔVt 就會被放大（本頁互動：naive 0.25 vs corrected 0.16，10 年外推相差超過 3 倍）。 這正是本站階段 5（原階段 4）規格頁裡「外推用的 n 必須來自應力中 OTF 擬合值，不能拿恢復後殘餘訊號反推」這條規則的完整物理依據。

檢查你懂了嗎

如果有人跟你說「我們量到 n=0.25，跟教科書上的 1/4 一致，所以是氫原子在擴散」，這個推論哪裡有問題？
這正是 Alam 等人指出的「pre-2003 核心難題」會重演的地方。單靠 n 值吻合不能下結論——還要看這個 n 是怎麼量出來的（有沒有延遲）、 活化能是否也同時指向同一個物種。Alam 的論點是：0.25 這個值很可能是延遲量測的假象，真正的擴散物種（H₂，n≈0.16）被量測方法蓋住了。 單一指標（n）吻合理論值，永遠不足以單獨證明機制正確，這跟階段 1 不要把單一理論值當通用常數的提醒是同一件事。
Grasser 說「n=0.15 吻合 1/6，不代表 R-D 模型正確」，他的論證邏輯是什麼？
他的邏輯不是否定那個數字，而是指出R-D 模型能同時正確預測應力與恢復兩個階段，才算真正驗證。 應力階段的 n=1/6 來自正反應與逆反應的精細平衡；如果這個平衡是真實的物理機制，它在恢復階段（逆反應主導）也該給出吻合實測的結果。 但實測恢復要跨 10 個十進位以上的時間，R-D 模型預測 3 個十進位內完成八成——同一套逆反應假設，在它本該最擅長的恢復階段反而失準， 這讓「應力階段數字吻合」的說服力大打折扣。
工程上不想選邊站，只想先把元件做出來，應該採取什麼立場？
兩個陣營都同意的部分，才是可以直接拿來用的工程結論：n 必須用應力期間（OTF 或等效方法）量到的值，絕不能用移除應力後的殘餘訊號反推； 而且要向量測方法本身要可信度——延遲時間、溫度相依性要一併報告，不能只給一個裸的 n 值。 至於「氫原子還是氫分子」、「R-D 模型對不對」這些還沒定論的問題，不影響你能不能把元件做對，但如果你的團隊要發展自己的加速模型， 最好知道業界連最基礎的擴散物種都還沒吵完，不要把任何單一論文的 n 值當成放諸四海皆準的常數。

⑤ What this means for you: why extrapolation can only use n from the stress phase

This threads Stages 2, 3, and 4 into one line: Stage 2 gave you the R-D theoretical values; Stage 3 told you measurement delay systematically underestimates the signal; this page tells you delay doesn't just shrink the number, it systematically pushes n upward too (because early data points lose a larger fraction to recovery, so the fitted slope comes out steeper). Put the three together and you get one clear engineering rule:

The n used for extrapolation can only come from fitting data taken during stress — never back-calculated from the residual signal after stress is removed
Whether you side with Alam/Mahapatra's "H₂ diffusion, the dispute is resolved" or Grasser/Kaczer's "the number being right doesn't mean the model is right," the two camps don't disagree on this one engineering rule: as long as the measurement method itself carries a delay, n gets systematically biased upward, and the 10-year extrapolated ΔVt gets amplified (this page's interactive: naive 0.25 vs corrected 0.16 — the 10-year extrapolations differ by more than 3×). This is the full physical justification behind this site's Stage 5 (formerly Stage 4) spec page's rule that "the n used for extrapolation must come from an OTF fit taken during stress, never back-calculated from residual post-recovery signal."

Check your understanding

If someone tells you "we measured n=0.25, which matches the textbook value of 1/4, so atomic hydrogen must be diffusing" — what's wrong with that inference?
This is exactly where the "pre-2003 core dilemma" Alam et al. identified would repeat itself. Matching n alone isn't enough to conclude anything — you also need to know how that n was measured (was there a delay?) and whether the activation energy independently points to the same species. Alam's argument is that 0.25 is very likely a delayed-measurement artifact, and the true diffusing species (H₂, n≈0.16) was masked by the measurement method. A single metric (n) matching a theoretical value is never sufficient on its own to prove a mechanism — the same lesson Stage 1 gives about not treating a single theoretical value as a universal constant.
Grasser says "n=0.15 matching 1/6 doesn't mean the R-D model is correct" — what's the logic behind that argument?
His logic isn't denying the number — it's that genuine validation requires the R-D model to correctly predict both the stress phase and the relaxation phase. The stress-phase n=1/6 comes from a delicate balance between the forward and reverse reactions; if that balance reflects real physics, it should also match measurement during relaxation (where the reverse reaction dominates). But measured relaxation spans more than 10 decades of time, while the R-D model predicts 80% completion within 3 decades — the very same reverse-reaction assumption fails precisely where it should shine, which substantially undercuts how persuasive the stress-phase number match really is.
If you're an engineer who just wants to build a working device without picking a side, what stance should you take?
What both camps agree on is the part you can put to direct engineering use: n must come from a value measured during stress (OTF or an equivalent method) — never back-calculated from residual signal after stress is removed; and you need to demand credibility from the measurement method itself — delay time and temperature dependence should be reported alongside it, not just a bare n value. As for whether it's atomic or molecular hydrogen, or whether the R-D model is right — those open questions don't block you from building a working device, but if your team is developing its own acceleration model, it's worth knowing the field hasn't even settled the most basic question of which species is diffusing, so don't treat any single paper's n value as a universal constant.
