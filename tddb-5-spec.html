階段 5 / 7

TDDB 的規格：JEDEC 怎麼規定、車規又加嚴了什麼

前三頁講的是物理與統計。這一頁講業界怎麼把它寫成可以驗收的條文 —— 以及一件很多人還不知道的事：TDDB 的 JEDEC 標準在 2024 年整個重組了。

先講結論

  • JESD263《Gate Dielectric Breakdown》（2024 年 3 月）已經取代 JESD35A、JESD35-1、JESD35-2 與 JESD92； 後四份在 2024 年 6 月同時被 rescinded（廢止）。還在引用 JESD92 的文件都該更新了。
  • TDDB 的規格語言是「在使用條件下、整顆晶片面積、十年內累積失效率 ≤ 某個 ppm」， 而不是像 HCI 那樣的「參數退化 10%」。
  • 量測方法分兩大類：晶圓層級的快速篩選（V-Ramp / J-Ramp，測 QBD、VBD） 與定電壓長時間應力（真正的 TDDB，測 tBD 分布）。前者管製程穩定度，後者才給壽命外推。
  • TDDB 的 Ea 是正的，所以高溫 HTOL（JESD47，125 °C / 1000 h）對它是有效加速 —— 這一點跟 HCI 完全相反。
  • AEC-Q100 沒有直接寫「TDDB 要撐幾年」。它定的是溫度等級與應力測試條件； 年限來自車廠的 mission profile。

兩類量測，用途完全不同

晶圓層級快篩：V-Ramp / J-Ramp
把電壓（或電流）一路拉上去直到崩潰，量 VBD 或 QBD（崩潰前總通過電荷）。
幾秒鐘一顆，可以在每一片 wafer 上量幾百點。
用途 製程穩定度監控、外質缺陷篩檢、批次比較。
不能 直接外推十年壽命 —— 崩潰時的電場遠高於使用電場，外推距離太遠。
定電壓 TDDB：tBD 分布
固定一個（或數個）應力電壓，量到崩潰的時間，得到 Weibull 分布的 β 與 η。
幾小時到幾百小時一組，每個條件要幾十顆才能定出 β。
用途 取得電壓與溫度加速參數，做壽命外推 —— 這才是規格數字的來源。
代價 慢、貴，而且應力電壓選太高會偏離使用條件的物理。

實務上兩者是搭配的：V-Ramp / J-Ramp 天天跑當製程哨兵，定電壓 TDDB 在製程定版與週期性可靠度驗證時做。

一份 TDDB 規格到底寫了什麼

TDDB 規格必須同時講清楚這六件事
① 使用條件Vuse（含 overdrive / 突波）與 Tuse。少寫溫度，數字沒有意義。
② 面積產品的閘極總面積（不是測試電容面積）。差幾個數量級的東西不能省略。
③ 目標壽命10 年？15 年？加上有效工作時數（duty cycle）。
④ 目標失效率例如 ≤ 100 ppm（商規）或 ≤ 1 ppm（車規）。這是 TDDB 規格的核心量。
⑤ 加速模型與參數E-model / 1-E / 冪次律，以及 γ、G、n、Ea。沒寫模型的壽命數字無法查核。
⑥ 信心水準例如「60% 信心下」。樣本數有限，點估計必須配信心區間。
對照一下 HCI。HCI 的規格是「VD = 某值、10 年後 ΔIdsat ≤ 10%」—— 一個參數門檻。TDDB 的規格是「10 年後失效率 ≤ 1 ppm」—— 一個機率。 兩者不能互相翻譯，因為背後的失效型態根本不同（連續漂移 vs 一次性崩潰）。 這也是為什麼 TDDB 報告一定要附 Weibull 圖，而 HCI 報告附的是退化曲線。

Stage 5 / 7

TDDB specs: what JEDEC requires, and what automotive grade tightens

The previous three pages covered physics and statistics. This page covers how industry turns that into acceptance-testable requirements — plus something many people still don't know: the JEDEC standards for TDDB were completely reorganized in 2024.

The bottom line first

  • JESD263 "Gate Dielectric Breakdown" (March 2024) has replaced JESD35A, JESD35-1, JESD35-2, and JESD92; all four of the latter were rescinded simultaneously in June 2024. Any document still citing JESD92 needs updating.
  • TDDB specs speak in "cumulative failure rate ≤ some ppm within ten years, at use condition, over the whole chip's gate area", not "10% parameter degradation" the way HCI does.
  • Measurement splits into two broad categories: fast wafer-level screening (V-Ramp / J-Ramp, measuring QBD, VBD) and constant-voltage long-duration stress (the real TDDB test, measuring the tBD distribution). The former monitors process stability; only the latter feeds lifetime extrapolation.
  • TDDB's Ea is positive, so high-temperature HTOL (JESD47, 125 °C / 1000 h) is an effective accelerated test for it — the exact opposite of HCI.
  • AEC-Q100 doesn't directly state "how many years TDDB must survive." It defines temperature grades and stress-test conditions; the actual year target comes from the automaker's mission profile.

Two kinds of measurement, completely different purposes

Wafer-level fast screen: V-Ramp / J-Ramp
Ramp voltage (or current) up until breakdown, measuring VBD or QBD (total charge passed before breakdown).
Seconds per device — hundreds of points can be measured on a single wafer.
Used for process-stability monitoring, extrinsic-defect screening, lot-to-lot comparison.
Cannot extrapolate a ten-year lifetime directly — the field at breakdown is far above the use field, so the extrapolation distance is too large.
Constant-voltage TDDB: the tBD distribution
Hold one (or several) stress voltages fixed and measure the time to breakdown, obtaining the Weibull β and η.
Hours to hundreds of hours per set, needing dozens of devices per condition to pin down β.
Used for obtaining voltage and temperature acceleration parameters for lifetime extrapolation — this is where spec numbers actually come from.
Cost slow and expensive, and choosing too high a stress voltage drifts away from use-condition physics.

In practice the two are paired: V-Ramp / J-Ramp runs daily as a process sentinel, while constant-voltage TDDB is done at process qualification and periodic reliability audits.

What a TDDB spec actually has to state

A TDDB spec must cover all six of these at once
① Use conditionVuse (including overdrive / surges) and Tuse. Leave out temperature and the number is meaningless.
② AreaThe product's total gate area (not the test capacitor's area). Something that differs by orders of magnitude can't be omitted.
③ Target lifetime10 years? 15 years? Plus the effective duty cycle.
④ Target failure rateE.g. ≤ 100 ppm (commercial) or ≤ 1 ppm (automotive). This is the core quantity in a TDDB spec.
⑤ Acceleration model & parametersE-model / 1/E / power law, plus γ, G, n, Ea. A lifetime number with no stated model cannot be verified.
⑥ Confidence levelE.g. "at 60% confidence." Sample sizes are limited, so a point estimate must come with a confidence interval.
Compare this to HCI. HCI's spec reads "at VD = some value, ΔIdsat ≤ 10% after 10 years" — a parameter threshold. TDDB's spec reads "failure rate ≤ 1 ppm after 10 years" — a probability. The two can't be translated into each other, because the underlying failure modes are fundamentally different (continuous drift vs. one-shot breakdown). That's also why a TDDB report always comes with a Weibull plot, while an HCI report comes with a degradation curve.

檢查你懂了嗎

同事拿一份 2019 年寫的可靠度報告，引用 JESD92 作為 TDDB 的依據。要更新嗎？
要。JESD92 已於 2024 年 6 月 rescinded，由 2024 年 3 月發行的 JESD263《Gate Dielectric Breakdown》 取代，同時被取代的還有 JESD35A、JESD35-1、JESD35-2。報告的物理與方法未必要改（定電壓應力仍然是定電壓應力），但引用的文件編號必須更新，而且應該回頭確認 JESD263 有沒有引入新的判定準則。
為什麼 V-Ramp 的 VBD 資料很漂亮，卻不能拿來保證十年壽命？
因為 V-Ramp 在遠高於使用電場的條件下崩潰（通常 10 MV/cm 以上），而使用電場可能只有 4～6 MV/cm。從那裡外推到十年，等於把階段 4 的「加速模型分歧」放大到極致 —— 不同模型可以差 10 個數量級以上。V-Ramp 的價值在相對比較（這批 vs 上批、這台機台 vs 那台），不在絕對壽命。
HTOL（125 °C / 1000 h）對 TDDB 和 HCI 的意義為什麼相反？
因為活化能的正負號相反。TDDB 的 Ea ≈ +0.75 eV，高溫加速崩潰，所以 125 °C 的 HTOL 對 TDDB 是真的在加速。HCI 的 Ea 是負的（低溫更嚴重），125 °C 反而是 HCI 最溫和的條件之一 —— HTOL 過了不代表 HCI 沒問題。同一份 HTOL 資料，對兩個機制的解釋完全不同。
Burn-in 能不能改善 TDDB？
要分本質與外質。外質失效（針孔、微粒、電漿損傷造成的局部薄點）壽命很短，burn-in 的高溫高壓確實會把它們燒出來，出貨前就被篩掉 —— 這是 burn-in 的主要價值。本質 TDDB 的壽命遠長於 burn-in 時間，burn-in 只消耗掉可忽略的一小段，改善不了。而且 burn-in 本身就在累積 TDDB 損傷，條件太兇反而會傷到好品。
身為 foundry，客戶問「你們這個製程的閘極氧化層可以撐幾年？」，該怎麼回答？
不能直接給年數 —— 那個數字取決於客戶自己的使用電壓、溫度、閘極總面積與目標失效率，這四項你都不知道。正確的交付是模型與分布：Weibull β 與 η、加速模型形式與參數（γ / n / G、Ea）、各參數的適用範圍、面積縮放的驗證資料，以及製程監控證明外質缺陷受控。再附上一組「典型條件下的示範計算」讓客戶知道怎麼用。這跟 HCI 的邏輯完全一樣：foundry 交模型，不交結論。

如何防治
模型範圍：滲流以簡化的立方格點模型模擬，Weibull 斜率由模擬本身湧現；加速模型（E-model / 1-E model / 冪次律）採文獻常見形式與參數量級。本教材用於呈現物理因果與統計行為，不是校準過的製程模型，也不能取代實測與 TCAD。
隱私：純靜態頁面，全部運算在你的瀏覽器本機執行 —— 無後端、無 cookie、無追蹤。教育用途，使用者自負驗證責任。
Notice how the verdict flips. Moving from commercial grade (Grade 3 temperature + 100 ppm) to automotive grade (Grade 0 temperature + 1 ppm), lifetime gets cut twice — once by temperature and once by failure rate. The size of the failure-rate cut is entirely determined by β — which is to say, by tox. For a thin-oxide process to reach automotive grade, the hard part usually isn't the average lifetime — it's the tail of the distribution.

Also required: process monitoring data (V-Ramp / J-Ramp wafer maps and trends) proving extrinsic defects are under control. JESD263, JEP122H
IC design Computes the circuit's own actual total gate area and the real voltage stress at each node (including overdrive, power-up surges, charge-pump nodes), and plugs these into the foundry's model to get a failure rate. PDK reliability model
Product / quality HTOL and burn-in. This is the layer where TDDB is actually visible — high temperature and high voltage genuinely accelerate it, and burn-in is specifically used to burn out extrinsic early failures. JESD47, AEC-Q100

The key difference from HCI: HCI is nearly invisible in product-level testing (its Ea is negative), while TDDB genuinely does get accelerated during HTOL and burn-in. So TDDB has a tool HCI doesn't — burn-in can screen out extrinsic failures, though it has no effect on intrinsic TDDB (the intrinsic lifetime is still far away; burn-in only consumes a negligible sliver of it).

Check your understanding

A colleague has a 2019 reliability report citing JESD92 as the basis for TDDB. Does it need updating?
Yes. JESD92 was rescinded in June 2024, replaced by JESD263 "Gate Dielectric Breakdown" (published March 2024), which also replaced JESD35A, JESD35-1, and JESD35-2. The report's physics and method don't necessarily need to change (constant-voltage stress is still constant-voltage stress), but the cited document numbers must be updated, and it's worth checking whether JESD263 introduced any new pass/fail criteria.
Why can't beautiful V-Ramp VBD data be used to guarantee a ten-year lifetime?
Because V-Ramp breaks down under a field far above the use field (usually above 10 MV/cm), while the use field might only be 4–6 MV/cm. Extrapolating from there to ten years pushes Stage 4's "acceleration-model divergence" to its extreme — different models can disagree by more than 10 orders of magnitude. V-Ramp's value is in relative comparison (this lot vs. the last one, this tool vs. that one), not absolute lifetime.
Why does HTOL (125 °C / 1000 h) mean opposite things for TDDB and HCI?
Because their activation energies have opposite signs. TDDB's Ea ≈ +0.75 eV, so high temperature accelerates breakdown, meaning 125 °C HTOL is genuinely accelerating for TDDB. HCI's Ea is negative (worse at low temperature), so 125 °C is actually one of HCI's gentlest conditions — passing HTOL says nothing about whether HCI is fine. The same HTOL data means something completely different for the two mechanisms.
Can burn-in improve TDDB?
You have to separate intrinsic from extrinsic. Extrinsic failures (local thin spots from pinholes, particles, plasma damage) have short lifetimes, and burn-in's high temperature and voltage genuinely burn them out before shipment — that's burn-in's main value. Intrinsic TDDB's lifetime is far longer than the burn-in duration, so burn-in only consumes a negligible sliver of it and can't improve it. And burn-in itself accumulates TDDB damage — push the conditions too hard and it can actually damage good parts.
As a foundry, a customer asks "how many years can your process's gate oxide last?" How should you answer?
You can't give a year number directly — that number depends on the customer's own use voltage, temperature, total gate area, and target failure rate, none of which you know. The correct deliverable is a model and a distribution: Weibull β and η, the acceleration model's form and parameters (γ / n / G, Ea), each parameter's valid range, area-scaling verification data, and process-monitoring evidence that extrinsic defects are controlled. Attach a worked example under typical conditions so the customer knows how to use it. The logic is exactly the same as HCI: the foundry delivers a model, not a conclusion.
