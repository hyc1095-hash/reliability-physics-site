MOSFET 熱載子注入（HCI）
可靠度物理，從零開始

手機用個兩三年、GPU 全速跑久了，電晶體會悄悄「變鈍」——這就是熱載子注入（Hot Carrier Injection）在做的事，也是晶片可靠度裡最常見的漸進式老化機制之一。 這份教材把它拆成七個階段：先靠一根滑桿建立直覺，再看實際發生在元件裡的機制，然後才是可以動手推的模型與公式， 接著是業界怎麼把物理量化成規格，最後給你一個可以隨便調的元件模型玩到懂為止。每一頁都短，一次只講一件事，卡住了隨時可以往回翻。

30 秒版本

MOSFET 導通時，電子從源極流向汲極。當汲極電壓夠高，電子在快要抵達汲極的那一小段路裡， 會被電場加速到遠超過室溫下該有的能量——這種電子稱為「熱」載子，「熱」指的是能量高，不是溫度高。

一部分熱電子會撞出新的電子電洞對、一部分會直接衝進閘極氧化層、 還有一部分會打斷矽與氫之間的鍵結。三件事都在通道靠近汲極的那一小塊區域發生， 留下的缺陷是永久性的——這就是為什麼 HCI 造成的是「回不去」的漂移，不是暫時的偏移。

MOSFET Hot Carrier Injection (HCI)
reliability physics, from zero

After a couple of years of daily use, or a GPU run flat-out for long stretches, transistors quietly get "duller." That's hot carrier injection at work — one of the most common gradual aging mechanisms in chip reliability. This course breaks it into seven stages: build intuition with a single slider, watch the mechanism actually happen inside the device, then move to a model and formula you can derive by hand, then see how industry turns that physics into a spec, and finish with a fully tunable device model to play with until it clicks. Every page is short and covers exactly one idea — you can always flip back.

The 30-second version

When a MOSFET is on, electrons flow from source to drain. If the drain voltage is high enough, electrons picking up speed in the last short stretch before the drain get accelerated by the field to far more energy than room temperature would give them — these are "hot" carriers. "Hot" means high-energy, not high-temperature.

Some of these hot electrons knock loose new electron-hole pairs, some plow straight into the gate oxide, and some break Si–H bonds at the interface. All three happen in the same small region near the drain, and the damage they leave behind is permanent — which is why HCI causes drift that never recovers, unlike a temporary shift.
