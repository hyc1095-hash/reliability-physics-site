階段 4 / 4 · 統計外推與防治

統計沿用同一套邏輯，防治分三層

統計外推：對數常態，理由跟 NBTI 一樣

EM 失效時間分布同樣採用對數常態，不是 Weibull —— 理由跟本站 TDDB 章節區分兩種分布時用的 「乘性退化 vs 極值分布」是同一把尺：單一導線的空洞成長是連續的物理過程，不是大量獨立區塊取最小值的滲流行為。

外推式 t(F) = t50 · exp( σ · z(F) ) z(F)：標準常態分布的反函數（quantile）。σ：典型落在 0.3–0.6 之間， 製程穩定度與晶粒結構均勻度是主要影響因子（呼應階段 2 材料表裡「晶粒結構決定 Ea」的結論 —— 晶粒結構不均勻，σ 也會跟著變大）。

防治：製程 / 版圖 / 材料三層

製程層

材料升級（見階段 2 表格）

Al → Al-Cu → Cu，介電質 cap → 金屬 cap（CoWP／CoSnP）。每一步都是把當時 Ea 最低的擴散路徑升級。

代價：金屬 cap 製程複雜度與成本增加，且會小幅提高線的 RC（電阻電容乘積）。
版圖層

Wide-narrow rule 與 redundant via

關鍵訊號線避免從寬線直接收窄到窄線（收窄處局部電流密度突增，且晶粒結構在收窄處容易變成純多晶， 活化能最差）；重要 via 用多個並聯 via 分攤電流，任一個 via 局部失效不會立即斷路。

代價：面積開銷，redundant via 在高密度先進節點不一定有空間放。
電路層

電流密度設計規則 + 動態電流分散

EDA 工具依 Black 方程式與製程 PDK 提供的 Jmax（依 Ea、n、目標壽命反推）做規則檢查； 時脈樹、電源網路等高電流密度線段優先分散到多層金屬、多條並聯路徑。

代價：更嚴格的 Jmax 規則會限制線寬縮減的空間，跟先進節點的密度目標互相拉扯。

與其他機制的關係

EM 是後段（BEOL）機制，HCI／TDDB／NBTI 是前段（電晶體本身）機制 —— 兩邊在物理位置上是分開的， 但共用同一個溫度軸：自我發熱造成的局部 ΔT 會同時加速前段的 NBTI／TDDB 與後段的 EM （見下一個模組「自我發熱耦合」）。

檢查你懂了嗎

為什麼 EM 的統計外推邏輯可以直接沿用 TDDB 章節區分 Weibull 的判斷標準？
因為那把尺本身是通用的物理判斷，不是 TDDB 專屬的：問「這個失效機制是連續退化到某個門檻（乘性退化） 還是等一個隨機事件發生（極值 / 滲流）」，答案決定該用對數常態還是 Weibull。EM 的空洞成長、NBTI 的界面態累積 都是前者；TDDB 的介電層貫穿路徑是後者。同一把尺套三次，答案不一樣是因為機制本質不同，不是這把尺失靈。
Wide-narrow rule 為什麼特別提到「收窄處容易變成純多晶」？
線寬變窄到跟晶粒尺寸接近或更小時，橫截面上能塞進去的完整晶粒變少， 結構會從多晶偏向竹狀（bamboo，晶粒橫跨整個線寬）。奇怪的是竹狀結構通常對 EM更有利 （晶界擴散路徑變少），但收窄處往往是製程應力集中、電流密度局部升高的地方， 兩個效應誰主導要看實際製程，不能只靠「竹狀結構比較好」一概而論。

Stage 4 / 4 · Statistical Extrapolation & Mitigation

Statistics reuses the same logic; mitigation splits into three layers

Statistical extrapolation: lognormal, for the same reason as NBTI

EM failure-time distributions likewise use lognormal, not Weibull — for the same reason as the "multiplicative degradation vs. extreme-value distribution" ruler this site's TDDB chapter uses to tell the two apart: void growth in a single wire is a continuous physical process, not percolation-style behavior where the minimum is taken over a large number of independent blocks.

Extrapolation formula t(F) = t50 · exp( σ · z(F) ) z(F): the inverse (quantile function) of the standard normal distribution. σ: typically 0.3–0.6, with process stability and grain-structure uniformity as the main drivers (echoing Stage 2's material-table conclusion that "grain structure determines Ea" — a less uniform grain structure also pushes σ higher).

Mitigation: three layers — process, layout, material

Process layer

Material upgrades (see the Stage 2 table)

Al → Al-Cu → Cu, dielectric cap → metal cap (CoWP / CoSnP). Every step upgrades whichever diffusion path currently has the lowest Ea.

Cost: a metal cap adds process complexity and cost, and slightly raises the wire's RC (resistance-capacitance product).
Layout layer

The wide-narrow rule and redundant vias

Avoid transitioning critical signal lines directly from a wide to a narrow width (the narrowing spot sees a local current-density spike, and grain structure there tends to turn pure polycrystalline, which is worst for activation energy); use multiple parallel vias for important connections so a single via's local failure doesn't immediately open the circuit.

Cost: area overhead — redundant vias don't always have room in a high-density advanced node.
Circuit layer

Current-density design rules + dynamic current spreading

EDA tools run rule checks against a Jmax derived from the Black equation and the process PDK (back-calculated from Ea, n, and the target lifetime); high-current-density segments like clock trees and power grids are preferentially spread across multiple metal layers and multiple parallel paths.

Cost: a stricter Jmax rule constrains how much line width can shrink, pulling against advanced-node density targets.

Relationship to the other mechanisms

EM is a back-end-of-line (BEOL) mechanism; HCI/TDDB/NBTI are front-end mechanisms (in the transistor itself) — the two are physically separate, but share the same temperature axis: local ΔT from self-heating accelerates both the front-end NBTI/TDDB and the back-end EM at once (see the next module, "self-heating coupling").

Check your understanding

Why can EM's statistical-extrapolation logic directly reuse the TDDB chapter's criterion for choosing Weibull?
Because that ruler itself is a general physical judgment, not specific to TDDB: ask "does this failure mechanism degrade continuously toward some threshold (multiplicative degradation), or does it wait for a single random event (extreme value / percolation)?" — the answer decides whether to use lognormal or Weibull. EM's void growth and NBTI's interface-trap accumulation are both the former; TDDB's dielectric percolation path is the latter. The same ruler applied three times gives different answers because the mechanisms differ in nature, not because the ruler fails.
Why does the wide-narrow rule specifically mention that "a narrowing spot tends to turn pure polycrystalline"?
When line width narrows to near or below the grain size, fewer complete grains fit across the cross-section, and the structure shifts from polycrystalline toward bamboo (where a single grain spans the full line width). Oddly, bamboo structure is usually better for EM (fewer grain-boundary paths), but a narrowing spot is often also where process stress concentrates and current density spikes locally — which effect dominates depends on the actual process, and can't be settled just by saying "bamboo structure is better."
