階段 2 / 4 · 物理機制與 R-D 模型

Si–H 鍵斷裂，反應與擴散的耦合

負偏壓下電洞在界面累積，界面處鈍化用的 Si–H 鍵在電場與電洞共同作用下斷裂， 釋出的氫物種擴散進閘極介電層，留下的懸鍵就是界面態 Nit —— 這是一個 反應（介面 Si-H 斷裂）＋ 擴散（H 物種離開介面）耦合的過程，稱為 R-D（Reaction-Diffusion）模型。

R-D 時間律 ΔVt(t) = A · tn A：前置係數，強烈製程相依。n：時間指數。 擴散限制（diffusion-limited）理論給出 n = 1/6 ≈ 0.167（H₂ 分子擴散）或 n = 1/4（H 原子擴散）。
實測落差

大量文獻室溫平面元件實測 n 落在 0.15–0.25 之間，跟理論值 1/6 同一量級但不精確吻合； 先進節點與 high-k／metal gate 疊層常見 n 偏高（0.2 附近），跟界面／介電層缺陷態密度、氮化製程有關。 跟本站 HCI 章節處理幸運電子指數 m 的原則一致：不要把單一理論值當成放諸四海皆準的常數，要標示實測範圍。

Stage 2 / 4 · Physical Mechanism & the R-D Model

Si–H bond breaking: reaction coupled to diffusion

Under negative bias, holes accumulate at the interface, and the Si–H bonds that passivate it break under the combined action of the field and those holes. The released hydrogen species diffuse into the gate dielectric, and the dangling bonds left behind are the interface traps Nit — this is a process where reaction (Si-H breaking at the interface) couples with diffusion (H species leaving the interface), known as the R-D (Reaction-Diffusion) model.

R-D time law ΔVt(t) = A · tn A: a prefactor, strongly process-dependent. n: the time exponent. Diffusion-limited theory gives n = 1/6 ≈ 0.167 (H₂ molecular diffusion) or n = 1/4 (atomic H diffusion).
The gap with measurement

A large body of literature on room-temperature planar devices measures n in the range 0.15–0.25 — the same order of magnitude as the theoretical 1/6, but not an exact match; advanced nodes and high-k/metal-gate stacks commonly see n run higher (near 0.2), related to interface/dielectric defect-state density and the nitridation process. This follows the same principle this site's HCI chapter uses for the lucky-electron exponent m: don't treat a single theoretical value as a universal constant — report the measured range.

檢查你懂了嗎

為什麼先進節點 high-k/metal gate 的 n 常偏高，接近 0.2 而不是 1/6？
n 值反映的是哪種微觀過程在限制動力學。理論 n=1/6 假設純擴散限制、界面缺陷態密度均勻； 先進節點的界面／介電層缺陷密度較高、缺陷分布也更不均勻，使得動力學偏離純擴散限制的理想情況， 表現為實測 n 值偏離理論值。這跟 HCI 章節裡幸運電子指數 m 在 28nm HKMG 實測只有 1.1（遠低於理論 2.85） 是同一類現象 ——疊層界面的缺陷物理會系統性地把實測值推離簡化模型的理論值。
R-D 模型的「反應」跟「擴散」分別對應哪個物理量？
「反應」是介面處 Si-H 鍵斷裂生成界面態與氫原子的化學反應速率；「擴散」是生成的氫物種 （原子或分子）離開界面、往閘極介電層內部擴散的速率。兩者耦合的意思是：如果氫物種擴散得不夠快、 在界面附近堆積，會提高局部氫濃度、抑制反應繼續往前（化學平衡往回推）—— 這正是為什麼純反應限制（n=1，線性）和純擴散限制（n=1/6 或 1/4）是兩個極端， 實際系統通常介於中間，且隨應力時間從反應限制段過渡到擴散限制段。

Check your understanding

Why is n on advanced-node high-k/metal-gate stacks often high, near 0.2 rather than 1/6?
The value of n reflects which microscopic process is limiting the kinetics. Theoretical n=1/6 assumes pure diffusion-limited behavior with a uniform interface defect-state density; advanced nodes have a higher interface/dielectric defect density with a less uniform distribution, pushing the kinetics away from the ideal pure-diffusion-limited case and showing up as a measured n that deviates from theory. This is the same class of phenomenon as the HCI chapter's lucky-electron exponent m measuring only 1.1 on 28nm HKMG (far below the theoretical 2.85) — defect physics at stacked interfaces systematically pushes measured values away from a simplified model's theoretical prediction.
What physical quantities do "reaction" and "diffusion" correspond to in the R-D model?
"Reaction" is the chemical reaction rate at which interfacial Si-H bonds break to generate interface traps and hydrogen atoms; "diffusion" is the rate at which the generated hydrogen species (atomic or molecular) leave the interface and diffuse into the gate dielectric. The coupling between the two means: if the hydrogen species doesn't diffuse away fast enough and piles up near the interface, it raises the local hydrogen concentration and suppresses the reaction from proceeding further (pushing the chemical equilibrium back) — this is exactly why pure reaction-limited (n=1, linear) and pure diffusion-limited (n=1/6 or 1/4) are two extremes, and a real system usually sits somewhere in between, transitioning from the reaction-limited regime to the diffusion-limited regime as stress time goes on.
