機制 05 · 串聯前面所有機制

自我發熱與熱載子的交互作用

這一頁不是新機制，是把前面四個模組的溫度活化能放到同一張表上一起看。 自我發熱造成的局部溫升 ΔT，同時對 HCI、NBTI、TDDB、EM 起作用 —— 但方向不一致， 這正是為什麼可靠度必須聯合最佳化，不能用一組 HTOL 溫度條件涵蓋全部機制。

一分鐘版

同一次應力、同一個 ΔT，HCI 的活化能是負的（升溫反而抑制）；NBTI、TDDB、EM 的活化能都是正的 （升溫加速劣化）。四個機制裡只有 HCI 是異數 —— 這在先進節點自我發熱量級動輒破百 K 時， 不再是可以忽略的小擾動。

關鍵直覺

同一顆元件、同一次應力，自我發熱把 HCI 往「看起來沒事」的方向推， 同時把 NBTI／TDDB／EM 往「加速劣化」的方向推 —— 這正是為什麼單一機制各自建模、 最後不放回同一個溫度軸看，會漏掉最關鍵的交互作用。

反饋迴路：功耗 → 溫升 → 機制分流

自我發熱 ΔT = Rth · P = Rth · ID · VD 局部溫升一旦產生，四個機制同時收到這個溫度變化，但反應方向不同 —— 這不是一個需要「解」的閉環（自我發熱本身不會因劣化而顯著改變，是單向的功耗到溫度）， 而是一個分流節點：同一個 ΔT 輸入，四條輸出各自沿自己的活化能方向移動。

實務上意味著：用同一組 HTOL 溫度條件去同時涵蓋這四個機制，本身就有方法學上的矛盾 —— 這呼應本站 HCI 章節「HCI/TDDB 溫度方向相反 → HTOL 只抓得到其中一個」的結論， 現在要再加上 NBTI 與 EM 的方向確認：四者中有三個（NBTI、TDDB、EM）方向一致，只有 HCI 是唯一的異數。

為什麼先進節點下這個耦合更嚴重

本站 HCI 章節記錄了 GAA sub-10nm 的自我發熱 ΔT 可達 100 K 以上，遠高於 14nm bulk FinFET 的個位數到十幾度。 當 ΔT 量級從「個位數」變成「三位數」，原本可以近似成小擾動修正的溫度效應， 會直接把元件推到一個對 NBTI／TDDB／EM 而言明顯加速、對 HCI 而言明顯抑制的工作點 —— 四個機制的淨風險排序可能因此在先進節點整個翻轉，這是純平面元件時代不太需要認真處理的問題。

Mechanism 05 · Ties Everything Before It Together

How Self-Heating Interacts With the Hot-Carrier Chain

This page isn't a new mechanism — it puts the temperature activation energies from the previous four modules on the same table so you can see them side by side. The local temperature rise ΔT from self-heating acts on HCI, NBTI, TDDB, and EM at the same time — but not in the same direction, which is exactly why reliability has to be jointly optimized, and why a single HTOL temperature condition can't cover every mechanism at once.

One-minute version

Under the same stress and the same ΔT, HCI's activation energy is negative (heating actually suppresses it); NBTI's, TDDB's, and EM's are all positive (heating accelerates degradation). HCI is the only outlier among the four mechanisms — and once self-heating in advanced nodes routinely exceeds 100 K, that's no longer a small perturbation you can afford to ignore.

Key intuition

On the same device, under the same stress, self-heating pushes HCI toward "looking fine" while simultaneously pushing NBTI/TDDB/EM toward "accelerated degradation" — which is exactly why modeling each mechanism in isolation, without ever putting them back on the same temperature axis, misses the single most important interaction.

The Feedback Loop: Power → Temperature Rise → Mechanism Split

Self-heating ΔT = Rth · P = Rth · ID · VD Once a local temperature rise appears, all four mechanisms receive the same temperature change — but they respond in different directions. This isn't a closed loop you need to "solve" (self-heating itself doesn't change appreciably from degradation; it's a one-way path from power to temperature) — it's a split node: the same ΔT input, with four outputs each moving along its own activation-energy direction.

In practice this means using a single HTOL temperature condition to cover all four mechanisms is methodologically contradictory on its own — echoing this site's HCI chapter conclusion that "HCI and TDDB point in opposite temperature directions, so HTOL only catches one of them." Now add the confirmation from NBTI and EM: three of the four (NBTI, TDDB, EM) point the same way, and HCI alone is the outlier.

Why This Coupling Gets Worse at Advanced Nodes

This site's HCI chapter documents self-heating ΔT exceeding 100 K in sub-10nm GAA devices, far above the single-to-low-double-digit range typical of 14nm bulk FinFETs. Once ΔT moves from a single digit to three digits, a temperature effect that used to be approximated as a small correction instead pushes the device straight to an operating point that's clearly accelerating for NBTI/TDDB/EM and clearly suppressing for HCI — the net risk ranking across the four mechanisms can flip entirely at advanced nodes, a problem planar-device-era engineering rarely had to take seriously.

誠實邊界

本頁的「反饋迴路」用詞其實是單向分流而非真正閉環回饋 —— 沒有把劣化本身回過頭改變 Rth 或功耗的二階效應納入（例如 Vt 漂移改變 ID、 進而改變 P），這在長期劣化下並非完全零效應，但本頁未建模。四機制的活化能都是文獻常見量級的代表值， 不是同一顆元件、同一次量測下取得的一致數據集。NBTI 對溫度的活化能方向本身在文獻上有一定分歧 （部分報告顯示更複雜的非單調行為），本頁採用最常見的簡化正向假設。GAA ΔT 可達 100K+ 的量級 引用本站 HCI 章節既有查證結果，本頁未重新查證，僅做延伸推論。

Honest boundary

This page's "feedback loop" language is really a one-way split, not a true closed-loop feedback — it doesn't include second-order effects where degradation itself feeds back into Rth or power (for example, Vt drift changing ID, which changes P). That effect isn't strictly zero under long-term degradation, but it isn't modeled here. The activation energies for all four mechanisms are representative literature-scale values, not a consistent dataset measured on the same device in the same run. The direction of NBTI's temperature activation energy itself has some disagreement in the literature (some reports show more complex, non-monotonic behavior); this page adopts the most common simplified positive-direction assumption. The claim that GAA ΔT can exceed 100K+ is carried over from this site's already-verified HCI chapter; it isn't independently re-verified here, only extended as inference.
